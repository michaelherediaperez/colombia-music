## What this is
A ~10-minute HTML presentation, **“Colombia: a country that sounds like its geography”**, by **Michael Heredia Pérez** (Colombian structural engineer, PhD student at Tongji University, Shanghai). It is his final presentation for the multinational class *General View of China*, where each student shows the music of their country.

Core argument: Colombia’s geography (three Andes ranges, two oceans, plains, Amazon, islands) isolated regions → each developed its own peoples and mix of Indigenous, African and European roots → each region has its own music, instruments and dances.

Audience: international classmates, presentation given in English. Michael writes to me in Spanish or English; answer in the language he uses, concise and collegial.

- Live (GitHub Pages, public repo): https://michaelherediaperez.github.io/colombia-music/ — the QR on the last slide points here. Later the deck may move into his personal site repo (`michaelherediaperez.github.io`), which is currently broken; if so, regenerate the QR (`tools/make-qr.mjs <url>`).
- A preview copy also lives as a claude.ai artifact; the repo is the source of truth.

## How it’s built
Plain HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no build step to present. Opening `index.html` from disk works offline.

```
index.html        slide markup (one <section class="slide"> per slide)
css/deck.css      styles + animations
js/deck.js        builds maps/grids/region slides + the presentation engine
js/media.js       every photo/sound path + audio button labels (edit names here)
js/mapdata.js     GENERATED by tools/build-maps.mjs — never edit by hand
media/            photos & audio (names listed in media/README.md)
img/qr.svg        QR code (tools/make-qr.mjs)
tools/            node scripts: npm install, npm run maps, npm run qr
```

Engine conventions (js/deck.js):
- Canvas is fixed 1920×1080, scaled to fit. Keys: →/Space next, ← back, F fullscreen, N speaker notes. URL hash `#n` = slide n.
- Click steps: any element with `data-at="k"` gets class `.on` at step k; `data-until="k"` hides it from step k. `.st` = animates in on its step; `.in` / `.pop` = animate on slide arrival with `--i` stagger.
- Speaker notes: `<aside class="notes">` inside each section (include rough timings).
- Photos: `.ph[data-photo=key]` shows a placeholder until `MEDIA.photo[key]` loads. Audio: `.aud[data-audio=key]` button; shows “add media/…” if the file is missing.
- Colombia maps come from DANE department borders (tools/data/dptos.geojson), Mercator-fitted to a 1000×1150 viewBox. All places/peoples are given as lon/lat in tools/build-maps.mjs and checked against the department they must fall in (warnings printed). Size comparisons use azimuthal equal-area projections at the same scale (true size).
- Typography: Fraunces (headings) + DM Sans (text), local woff2 in fonts/. Palette: ink #0F1B2D, paper #F5EFE3, gold #F4B41A, red #D64933; region/axis colours are in deck.js (NAT, AX).

## Facts and sources (keep sources on-slide as small footnotes)
- Musical axes: “Ejes musicales de Colombia”, Wikipedia (es).
- Instruments slide: colombia.co, “The sound of music: traditional Colombian instruments”.
- 115 Indigenous peoples: DANE, CNPV 2018. 65 Indigenous languages (68 native incl. 2 creoles + Romani): Ministerio de Cultura / MinEducación.
- UNESCO: Vallenato (urgent safeguarding, 2015); marimba music & chants of the South Pacific (2010, extended 2015); Colombian-Venezuelan llano work songs (urgent safeguarding, 2017); Coffee Cultural Landscape (Manizales region).
- Areas: Colombia ≈1.14 M km² ≈ Spain+France+Portugal ≈ Ethiopia; Africa ≈26 Colombias; China ≈8; Colombia ≈180× Shanghai municipality.
- Las Lajas is in Ipiales (Nariño), not Pasto. Tatacoa is in Villavieja, Huila.
Michael cares about accuracy: verify any new fact or location against an official/primary source before adding it, and add the source as a footnote.
